## EW200-Final-Pygame
# My final duck hunting game!
Come hunt for ducks in a multi-level, dynamic, exhilarating game! Immerse yourself in the sounds of hunting and see if you can beat the clock with good aim. Use your arrows to move the gun and the mousepad to shoot. Good luck!

# To be counted for points:
- mouse master- press mouse to shoot (+2)
- keyboard king- press keys to move gun (+2)
- tiler- background design of the shooting gallery (+2)
- sound blaster- gun shots and ducks getting hit (+2)
- level up- ducks speed up and increase in number each round (+4)
- physical- physics of the bullets shooting (+4)
- shooter- shoot the bullets (+2)
- textual- dynamic text (timer and bullet counter) (+2)
- shifting screens- intro screen, level screens, lost/won screens (+2)
- tick tock- time on levels (+2)
- fancy font- used an online font (+1)
- the walls are hard- gun cannot leave the screen (+4)
- muzak- play continuous background music (+2)
- looking weak/great graphics- ducks change to gray and fall when shot (+3/+4)
- points-r-us- track how many bullets have been shot per level (+2)
